Elsinore

The royal family is dead. The crown passes to you. Rule the city for one year, and every choice will answer for itself.

  1. Is justice attainable in the real world, and is there a timeline for it?
  2. When is grief a positive thing, and when does it become negative?
  3. How do we deal with the good and evil within us, and with fate we can't handle alone?

Fate rolls the die

How to win

Goal: rule Elsinore until the last month and finish with all four goals met. The goals and the length of your reign depend on the difficulty: Apprentice (12 months, easiest goals), Prince (12 months), King (18 months, harder goals and hard dilemmas) and Tragedy (24 months, the hardest goals, only 2 royal actions a month). Meet all four for the best ending, The just and healing crown. The goal bar under the meters shows your progress.

You lose early if Order, Soul or People reach 0, if the Treasury is still at 0 when a month ends, or if Norway's threat reaches 100 and their invasion breaks through your defenses.

Each month: fate rolls a 12-sided die, a character from Hamlet brings you a problem, and then you use the royal desk: 3 royal actions plus court hearings, businesses, voyages, buildings, laws, advisors and petitions.

Tips: Justice slips every month and gets harder to raise the higher it goes, so keep holding fair hearings. Grief fades when people grieve together and turns into buried grief when you rush them. Feed the city: food comes from the seasons, farms, fish and voyages to Poland. Some choices come back months later.

Each month

Fate rolls a 12-sided die. A character from Hamlet brings you a problem (press 1, 2 or 3). Then, at the royal desk (press 1 to 6 to switch tabs), you hear court cases, build, pass laws, choose advisors and answer petitions before ending the month.

Read the city

Rain and black banners mean grief, purple fog and crows mean corruption, golden light means a good soul, fires mean disorder, ships mean wealth. A line forms outside the courthouse when cases wait, and Norway's tents appear on the hills as their threat grows.

Losing the city

Your reign ends early if order, the treasury, the soul or the people fall to zero, or if Norway's threat reaches 100 and their invasion breaks through. Advisors you neglect may turn against you.
